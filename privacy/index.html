Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 23, 2026 · Last Updated: April 23, 2026

Your privacy matters. Divorce is one of the most personal moments in life, and we take the privacy of your information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to services operated by Divorce Angels Inc., a corporation based in Ontario, Canada ("we," "us," or "our"). Contact: mara@divorceangels.ca.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information — such as your name and email address when you register
  • Financial information you enter into the Service — for example, asset values, debts, income, and expenses you input into our tools
  • Documents you upload — such as legal letters or financial statements you ask our document analyzer to review
  • Conversations with our AI assistant — messages you send to "Angel" and the responses generated
  • Communications with us — such as emails you send to support
  • Payment information — when you subscribe, processed by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — how you interact with the Service, pages visited, features used, and time spent
  • Device data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 7 below

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • Generate personalized outputs, such as settlement scenarios and AI responses based on your inputs
  • Create and manage your account
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Send you transactional messages about your account and updates to the Service
  • Send marketing communications, where you have consented (you can opt out at any time)
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve the Service
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and similar frameworks, we process your personal information based on:

  • Your consent — which you provide by creating an account and using the Service
  • Contractual necessity — to deliver the Service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — such as improving the Service and preventing fraud
  • Legal obligations — where required by law

You can withdraw consent at any time by closing your account or contacting us, subject to any legal or contractual restrictions.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

With Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers to help us operate the Service. These providers have access to your information only to perform services on our behalf and are contractually bound to protect it. Our current providers include:

  • Netlify — website hosting and serverless functions
  • Supabase — user account database and authentication
  • Anthropic — AI processing that powers the Angel assistant
  • Stripe — payment processing (when paid features are active)
  • Google Analytics — usage and traffic analytics

Each provider has its own privacy policy, which governs how they process information on our behalf.

For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Divorce Angels Inc., our users, or others.

Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Canada (for example, in the United States). When your information is transferred internationally, it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions. We take steps to ensure your information remains protected, including through contractual safeguards.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may limit some features.

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns. Google Analytics may set cookies on your device. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information using administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. This includes encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Under Canadian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent to processing (subject to legal or contractual restrictions)
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Complain to a privacy regulator if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mara@divorceangels.ca. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required by law.

11. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you through the Service or by email. Your continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first at mara@divorceangels.ca. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at mara@divorceangels.ca.
