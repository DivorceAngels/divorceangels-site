<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I am two years into my Divorce and have
found that writing has been my greatest comfort and one of the only ways I have
been able to maintain my sanity.&nbsp; I have
decided to share my story with Divorce Angels with the hopes that I can provide
insight, help and awareness for those going through a Divorce or supporting
someone going through a Divorce.&nbsp; I
welcome your comments but remember, I am not the authority on Divorce and this
is just one girls story….</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The
Marriage</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>“Nick” and I were married in our late
20’s.&nbsp; We had been together for over two
years and although we didn’t have the perfect relationship, we certainly loved
each other and looked forward to spending the rest of our lives together.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Looking back there were many clues to
indicate how this relationship would end up but we were young and in love and
not paying attention.&nbsp; I always say that
marriages that go the distance are just dumb luck.&nbsp; How can anyone know what they want or will
need in a life partner in their 20’s? And what you want in your 20’s is
different than what you want/need in your 30’s, 40’s and 50’s.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I got pregnant on our honeymoon which was
not in the plans.&nbsp; I was finishing up my
teaching certification and had great plans for my future -&nbsp; none of which included having a baby.&nbsp; However we rallied around it and began our
family.&nbsp; We have 2 beautiful girls and I
am thankful every day for them.&nbsp; They are
the greatest product of our marriage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I gave up my dream of being a teacher and
became a stay at home mom.&nbsp; I didn’t even
think twice about it.&nbsp; I wanted to be
able to raise my kids and Nick and I decided that it made the most sense.&nbsp; In retrospect, I think he liked it because it
gave him ultimate control.&nbsp; I needed him
to take care of us and he thrived on being needed.&nbsp; I was so busy with the girls that I never
really stopped to notice or did I? He gave me a weekly allowance and a credit
card.&nbsp;&nbsp; Everything I did was monitored by
him.&nbsp; I don’t think this bothered me when
the girls were little, but as they grew up and didn’t need me as much, I had
issue.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I am not sure if I would call Nick a narcissist
although he exhibits many of the qualities but I would say he is extremely
difficult to live with.&nbsp; He would yell at
the girls and I if our shoes weren’t lined up at the front door, he would freak
if there was a glass in the sink.&nbsp; As he
got older he became more obsessive compulsive and there seemed to be a right
way (read “his way”) to do everything… we couldn’t even flush the toilet
correctly.&nbsp; When he would walk in the
door we would be terrified… fearful of setting him off and listening to his
rant.&nbsp; We walked on eggshells and our
home was not a happy place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Miserable, I knew I wanted to leave but I
am a child of Divorce and didn’t want the same fate for my girls.&nbsp; I hung on until finally I couldn’t take it
any more.&nbsp; I sat him down and told him it
was time.&nbsp; He agreed, he too was miserable
he just didn’t have the balls to call it.&nbsp;&nbsp;
The reality is that he had a huge ego with little self esteem and got
his way by being a bully.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was both relieved and scared.&nbsp; I knew money was going to be an issue and
that he would be difficult to deal with, but my girls were getting older and I
had to show them a better way.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Guess
What Girls – It’s OVER</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was convinced that telling the girls was
not going to be easy.&nbsp; I spent many
sleepless nights agonizing on what to say and when to do it.&nbsp; We couldn’t agree on a right time and it was
becoming such an issue that we just sat them down and told them.&nbsp; Their response was telling… &nbsp;They asked us what took so long? They knew we
were both unhappy and they were tired of living in an unhappy home.&nbsp; I was so proud of them for speaking their
mind and telling their father the effects he had on them.&nbsp; They told him that he spoke to all of us
rudely and that he was impossible to keep happy.&nbsp; I didn’t say a word.. I let them vent.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Of course Nick blamed me.&nbsp; I had poisoned the girls against him. I had over
shared.&nbsp; He didn’t want to take any
responsibility for his part or look at himself.&nbsp;
I was done fighting with him and just wanted to move on.&nbsp; I also wanted him to move out. J</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I assumed that I could make him leave the
house.&nbsp; That I could change the locks
like they did in the countless TV shows I had watched and that he would be
gone.&nbsp; Boy was I mistaken.&nbsp; Not only was he not planning on moving out, his
lawyer had advised him not to.&nbsp; The law
protects both spouses and both have “an equal right to possession of the
matrimonial home”.&nbsp; His lawyer? I was
shocked that he had moved so fast and couldn’t believe that he didn’t tell me
he was hiring someone.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I cannot tell you how crazy it is to be so
in love and part of someone for 20 years to feeling like you don’t know them at
all.&nbsp; I began to question everything&nbsp; about our past and that was not fun.&nbsp; I went over all our years together and every
scenario and suddenly saw it with a different perspective. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was soon to learn that everything I had
assumed about Nick would be incorrect , and that good old Nick was going to give
me a run for money.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>My
first visit to a Family Law Lawyer</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Given that Nick had already seen a lawyer
(hence the parenting plan) I knew I had to as well.&nbsp; Of course it wasn’t so easy for me.&nbsp; I did not have any of my own money and I had
limited access to our bank accounts.&nbsp; Yes
we had a joint account where Nick deposited money into but that was all I
had.&nbsp; I had no personal credit card and
the card I had from Nick had a $2500 limit.&nbsp;
Where was I going to get money to see or hire a lawyer? &nbsp;This was one of my biggest mistakes during our
marriage – but more about this topic later.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I didn’t want to but I had to go to my
mother and ask for help.&nbsp; I knew we owned
our house so I knew I could pay her back, but I was totally illiquid.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My best friend came with me to meet the
lawyer.&nbsp; I wasn’t sure I would be able to
take in all the information at the meeting and she was amazing.&nbsp; She came and asked all the questions and took
notes.&nbsp; I sat there like a deer in the
headlights.&nbsp; It felt surreal to me.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Don’t get me wrong, I was looking forward
to my relationship ending.&nbsp; I just
couldn’t imagine going through the whole Divorce process.&nbsp; And I was scared.&nbsp; Very scared about money and being taken care
of and what the future would hold for me and my girls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I don’t remember much from our initial
consultation other than he explained to me my options: Litigation, Mediation,
Arbitration, Collaborative Law.&nbsp; I was so
overwhelmed that my mind shut off.&nbsp; The
only real thing I remembered was that he wanted a $5000 retainer so that he
could begin and that his offices were big and fancy and I found him arrogant .</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I went home feeing overwhelmed.&nbsp; How did I know this lawyer was the one for
me? How do you ever know? I decided that I couldn’t make my decision based on
one meeting and felt I needed time.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So I went home and did my research.&nbsp; I first researched Nick’s lawyer to get an
understanding of how he was going to approach our Divorce.&nbsp; I then sat down with Nick and asked him how
HE intended to approach our Divorce.&nbsp;
Could we do this through mediation? Did we really have to fight? We both
wanted out and I just wanted a fair settlement. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I sat Nick down and said “Let’s do this
easily,&nbsp; Let’s be amicable”.&nbsp; I quickly learnt that amicable for Nick meant
he would be amicable as long as we did it his way. I realized that he was so
used to being the one in control in this relationship that achieving a fair
settlement on my own would be next to impossible. I knew then and there that I
was going to have to get it together and take Nick on as he believed that the
laws did not apply to him.&nbsp; I discussed
Child and Spousal Support Guidelines with him, showed him the online
calculators –&nbsp; he told me &nbsp;that they don’t apply to him.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I needed to find a good lawyer (that I
could afford) and quick.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>My
next visit to a Family Lawyer</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Recognizing that I had to get it together
and advocate for myself, I went on a quest to find a family lawyer that I was
comfortable with and that I could afford.&nbsp;
I asked friends and family for recommendations. &nbsp;I did research on each name I was given.&nbsp; I went to Divorce Angels (hence how I got to
the site and started this blog).&nbsp; I found
someone who was highly recommended and whose credentials checked out (on the
web at least) and booked a consultation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This time I went on my own.&nbsp; I was stronger and more focused and I was
ready to listen and plan.&nbsp; This lawyer
was more my speed.&nbsp; She was a sole practitioner
and her offices were quiet and calming.&nbsp;
I felt at ease as soon as I walked in to reception.&nbsp; After hearing my story, she agreed that we
had some obstacles to overcome but was quite confident we would get the right
results.&nbsp; I loved her honesty – she told
me she was not a therapist and that as much as she would be there for me it was
in my best interest to not use her that way.&nbsp;
She was mindful of my finances and really took the time to explain to me
how I could keep my costs down.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>During our initial meeting she advised me
to do a couple of things that I hadn’t even thought of.&nbsp; She had me change all my passwords for Social
Media, Banking and most importantly my Email.&nbsp;
</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I hired her and felt like I was in great
hands.&nbsp; I cannot tell you how comforting
that was for me.&nbsp; It is really important
to find someone you connect with.&nbsp; It
would have been a terrible mistake had I hired the first lawyer I saw.&nbsp; If I can offer any advice it would be to shop
around and interview a couple of people before you commit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Please
Get Out – Please!!</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nick and I were at a stalemate.&nbsp; Neither one of us would move out and neither
one of us would move into the spare bedroom.&nbsp;
How could we possibly be separated and not just living in the same house,
but the same bed? It was ridiculous and awkward but I couldn’t see another
way.&nbsp; I couldn’t force him to leave the
bedroom and I wasn’t leaving either.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the first time, I actually realized how
controlling Nick was and how I had given him so much power through our years
together.&nbsp;&nbsp; We were having the ultimate
power struggle and I was not going to be the first to blink.&nbsp; I used to do anything to keep him happy and
calm. Now I was doing anything I could to piss him off.&nbsp; It was a very unhealthy way to live.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was doing awful things that were so out
of my character.&nbsp; At first it was
thrilling and I definitely got caught up in the moment. But ultimately I was
just stooping to his level and wasn’t really considering my girls.&nbsp; I was ashamed of myself and knew that if I
was ever going to move on, I would need to move out.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The reality was that we could not afford to
keep the house and so I was delaying the inevitable.&nbsp; If Nick wanted to stay in the house while we
worked out the Divorce then he could.&nbsp; I
decided to take charge of my life (finally) and create a new home for my
kids.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We were both relieved that I called it,
even though Nick wouldn’t admit it.&nbsp; However
through our lawyers we worked out a temporary custody and financial agreement so
that I could actually move out.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Umm..
I have no credit?!</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As I am looking for places to move to, I
realize that I have no credit.&nbsp; I don’t
even have my own bank account.&nbsp; I don’t
know why but I had always assumed that as a secondary credit cardholder I was
obtaining credit.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I made an appointment at our bank and sat
across from a “twenty something” service rep who looked at me like I was insane
when I told her I didn’t have my own account or credit card.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>On
My Way</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once my finances and living situation was
sorted out and a schedule was in place for the kids, I finally felt like I
could move forward.&nbsp; I have stopped
focusing on my Divorce and am concentrating on myself and my kids.&nbsp; </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I hope my tips help someone else going
through a Divorce.&nbsp; I felt so overwhelmed
and truly had no idea where to begin.&nbsp; I
know it’s scary but I promise you – you will be okay after your Divorce.&nbsp; In fact, you will be better!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jacqueline</p>
