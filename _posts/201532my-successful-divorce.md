<p>There is such a thing as a successful divorce.&nbsp;</p><p>It's&nbsp;a process...&nbsp;a good investment into a future of new beginnings and a not forgotten past.</p><p>I became a divorcee at the age of 28 with two kids under the age of 3&nbsp;and a work ethic but no career.&nbsp;&nbsp;I will never forget the words my lawyer said to&nbsp;me &nbsp;"How will you supplement your income?" &nbsp; Those words did not register,&nbsp;not comprehensible at that time. &nbsp;</p><p>I quickly realized I&nbsp;needed a&nbsp;plan,&nbsp;a vision. &nbsp;How was I going to become&nbsp;an important citizen, a role model to my kids and create a path to a new beginning?&nbsp;It was all&nbsp;too overwhelming!</p><p>I was not sure how to enter back into the work force. &nbsp;Dealing with the emotional loss of my marriage, the reality of my future and raising two young boys seemed more than I could handle. &nbsp;&nbsp;I was schooled but had put all career opportunities on hold to raise my children. &nbsp;I knew that I had to take control of my life. &nbsp;Even though the steps were hard, I knew the example I set for my kids was crucial to both their development and mine. &nbsp;I had no choice but to&nbsp;create a life for myself.</p><p>There is an old saying that if you find something you love and are passionate about it,&nbsp;the money will follow. &nbsp;This is what happened to me.&nbsp; From my experience, the path you follow will turn into a road and that road will eventually open up to many streets and avenues. &nbsp; If you can find the belief in what you were meant to do, the opportunities are endless. &nbsp;The kids were my inspiration. The choice&nbsp;was mine.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>I created&nbsp;Peony, a decorating firm, &nbsp;with my friend and now business partner Ellen. &nbsp;We based our business&nbsp;on the premise of home office /home life.&nbsp;&nbsp;We were both raising small children and a home based business was the only way we could do both well&nbsp;at the same time.&nbsp;&nbsp;We also had the same philosophy that a house is not a home until you are surrounded by the&nbsp;things you love. &nbsp;This was comforting to me during the early divorce years - being in a safe and loving home made me feel confident and secure.&nbsp;&nbsp;Slowly, through building a new, comfortable environment I felt I could&nbsp;achieve my goals through the toughest of times.</p><p>Divorce is not a sentence. It's a book of many chapters. If you read along slow and steady you will get to the best parts of the story and hopefully have a very happy ending. &nbsp;In other words, if you push yourself toward developing your own self - and not getting stuck -&nbsp;you can learn to move past and develop a new, different and beautiful story.&nbsp;&nbsp;As I have!</p><p>Paula Seligman&nbsp;</p><p>Co owner of Peony Design,&nbsp;www.peonydesign.ca</p><p>Happily remarried for 17 years&nbsp;with 4 children</p>
