<p><span style="font-size:medium">We were driving to the courthouse for a morning of discovery.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">I was nervous and excited as I anticipated the moment that our eyes</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">would finally lock and we would chuckle at the ludicrous situation that we were in. &nbsp;</span><span style="font-size:medium">How did we even get here?</span></p><p><span style="font-size:medium">He would reach over try to engage me in conversation and we would laugh&nbsp;</span><span style="font-size:medium">at our folly. Nice delusional thought but the reality of our encounter blew the wick off my imaginary dream&nbsp;</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">with a gust of frigid truth.&nbsp;</span><br /> </p><p><span style="font-size:medium">We sat as adversaries flanked by our expensive guards.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">He stared through me with vacant eyes, game face on without a hint of emotion. &nbsp;</span><span style="font-size:medium">I began to fidget, positioning for some type of acknowledgement or connection.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">I mean, was I not still the Mother of his children?</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">But he was gone.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">Retreating to his cone of silence.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">Oh how I remember this.&nbsp; How many times had I been here before?</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">Isolated, lonely afraid to really feel the truth of our disconnection.</span><br /><span style="font-size:medium">Back in the car I cried, as if for the first time.&nbsp;
