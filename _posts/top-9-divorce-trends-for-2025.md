<h4 class="wp-block-heading">6. <strong>Adjusting to Post-Pandemic Realities</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The COVID-19 pandemic left a lasting mark on family dynamics and divorce trends. If you delayed separation during the pandemic, you’re not alone—many couples are now pursuing divorce, which has created a backlog in family courts. Virtual mediation and court proceedings, which became common during the pandemic, are here to stay and can be a convenient option for many.</p>
