<p>She was combing her hair when it happened.&nbsp; I didn’t see it coming.&nbsp; I thought we were in the middle of playing dress up or lego&nbsp;or some other kid game.&nbsp; She just hit me with it right between the eyes:&nbsp;</p><p>“Why did you and mommy divorce?”&nbsp;</p><p>I just stared at her, blankly…</p><p>I’ll save you the suspense. &nbsp;If you have a daughter and you are a single dad, and if the question hasn’t been asked yet, it’s coming.</p><p>Here are a few things <strong>NOT</strong> to say when that happens:</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>1.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Because mommy caught daddy sleeping with &lt;INSERT NAME OF MISTRESS HERE&gt;.</em></strong></p><p>Even if that’s the reason, sometimes the truth doesn’t set you free.&nbsp; It puts your kid in therapy for a long, long time.&nbsp; Don’t get me wrong, your daughter will eventually find this out, but let it happen at the right time, like on her prom night or just before you walk her down the aisle.</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>2.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Because mommy is a bitch.</em></strong></p><p>See my comments above.</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>3.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Sometimes adults fall out of love.</em></strong></p><p>I hate this one.&nbsp; You might as well say to her “sometimes adults are just fucked up in the head”.&nbsp; Both will have the same impact, and it won’t be a good one.&nbsp; Young girls believe in eternal love and that’s a nice fantasy for them to preserve for as long as possible.&nbsp; Don’t mess that up.&nbsp; They will have plenty of time to become jaded, angry and cynical&nbsp;– no need to accelerate the process.</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>4.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Because we were fighting over &lt;INSERT ONE OR MORE OF THESE WORDS: money, you bratty kids, religion, my parents, mommy’s parents, my work, mommy’s work, my unemployment, mommy’s unemployment, my halitosis, her weight problem, my buddies, her loser friends, etc.&gt;</em></strong></p><p>You get the idea.&nbsp; There are lots of “reasons” why your marriage ended.&nbsp; Don’t share any of them with your daughter.&nbsp; It will lead to more questions, and more nasty conversations with your ex when your daughter runs back to her to report what you just told her.&nbsp; Leave specifics out of this.</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>5.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Because daddy wanted some alone time.</em></strong></p><p>Laa-aame.&nbsp; If you want some alone time, go read a book or go for a fishing weekend.&nbsp; You don’t leave your wife of a dozen years because you need some alone time.&nbsp; And, any self-respecting 11-year old girl has already figured this out, even at that age.&nbsp; If you want to get stuck in an endless loop of “But why?” questions from her, use this answer.</p><p style="margin-left:18pt"><!----><strong><em>6.<span style="font-size:7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></em></strong><!----><strong><em>Because I only have to wait another 10 years before I can start dating your friends.</em></strong></p><p><span style="font-size:11.0pt">That one is not cooI. &nbsp;It gets you locked up in jail, you sick pervert</span><!--EndFragment--></p><p>In all seriousness, I have found that being open and honest with my daughter has been the best route. &nbsp;Keeping in mind that it is important that she feels protective of both her mom and I and&nbsp;needs to feel that there&nbsp;is no underlying anger between her parents. &nbsp;This has not been easy, but at least it is one thing my ex and I agree upon.</p><p>Newly Divorced Dad -- Father of 2</p><p> </p><p> </p>
