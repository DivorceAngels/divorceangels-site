<p>At 40, after years of trying to find “Mr. Right”, I finally found my perfect match.</p><p>It was a long haul… sifting through the many frogs, the horrible dates and the endless lonely nights… but when I found him, it was bliss.</p><p>Something about the hard journey that makes it that much sweeter when you actually find “the one” later in life … a little more appreciation, the insight into what it “takes” and the foresight to not sweat the small stuff.</p><p>I was literally elated. &nbsp;Finding this match was more than I had ever dreamed of!</p><p>I guess I had modified the fairy tale slightly by this point but still…it was the best thing that had ever happened to me. &nbsp;Except the tale never factored in his ex.</p><p>Nor had it included the nuances that came with being a stepmom and part of a blended family. &nbsp;We were starting a new life together but his old life wasn’t exactly gone.</p><p>They shared four children and her presence seemed to consume me. &nbsp;I saw her everywhere - every other weekend, a few nights a weeks and during the days without kids I still felt her out there.</p><p>I got lucky with the children - we hit it off right away and I truly fell in love with them. &nbsp;In the beginning, it was all affection…. I was so excited to have them around I couldn't help but spoil them… I became a camp counselor, the “Disneyland” parent.</p><p>But then reality set…</p><p>The kids got used to me… I was no longer the most exciting fun person on the planet. &nbsp;I got tired of making concessions so as to “not rock the boat” with the ex. &nbsp;I got frustrated by the millions of back and forth phone calls between homes.</p><p>And I got confused… confused by my role.</p><p>How do I make this work? &nbsp;How can I create a life with my husband that doesn’t negate his past. &nbsp;How can I do right by my step kids and respect their original family unit? &nbsp;Where exactly was my place???? &nbsp;Often an underrated role and sensationalized as the evil monster in movies and books, I felt insecure and unsure.</p><p>I poured through books and blogs and forums for the answers.&nbsp;I looked to friends who had already experienced this dynamic. &nbsp;But their stories seemed different… Most were blending two sets of kids with both sides bringing the same level set concerns… I was going at this alone.</p><p>I quickly realized it was going to be turbulent. &nbsp;</p><p>There was no way of getting around it… the family began before my time and I had to find a way to function within it, creating a new environment without feeling haunted by the past.</p><p>It was and still is tricky….</p><p>Often I stumble, get jolted, and even suffer from pangs of jealousy. &nbsp;&nbsp;Sometimes I’m not sure if my actions are appropriate - are they too involved or not enough?</p><p>But then my husband wraps his arms around me and I find my place. &nbsp;And I know I have to persevere.</p><p>Newlywed Step-Mom</p>
