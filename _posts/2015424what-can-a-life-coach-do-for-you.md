<p><br />The constant in life is change, yet we are frantically determined to hang on to the people<br />and things we love.</p><p>After a divorce or life transition the awareness of this impermanence is heightened.&nbsp;<br />Besides our partner we notice that certain people in our collective have<br />suddenly disappeared. Friends and family that once offered love and support may<br />now be absent in our time of need, perhaps triggered by their own fears and allegiances.<br />The lines are drawn and inevitably sides are picked.</p><p>As if losing your husband/wife isn't enough you now experience further isolation<br />and the totality of your aloneness. The weight of these losses can paralyze and<br />disable movement and zap your energy for life. Lost in an emotional blizzard it is hard<br />to find solid ground.&nbsp;</p><p>A Life Coach helps tether and support you through this storm.</p><p>Your Coach becomes your new "Plus One".&nbsp;&nbsp;The person you allow to see behind your mask.<br />The coach creates a safe, relaxing, inspiring space (either in person or via phone) where the client and coach work on a deep experiential and intuitive level to explore, heal, create and activate the life you deserve.</p><p>What is the difference between a Therapist and a Coach?</p><p>While they both offer valuable services they offer individuals support in different ways.<br />Counselling focuses on the PAST and helps break up issues or traumatic events that are<br />blocking the client from moving forward in their lives.&nbsp;&nbsp;It is often based on a diagnosis or concern.</p><p>The Therapist/Client relationship takes the form of "expert" and "patient".</p><p>Coaching focuses on the PRESENT and on moving forward into the future.<br />It helps people become "unstuck".&nbsp;&nbsp;It is based on the belief that the client is<br />healthy, creative, resourceful and whole.&nbsp;&nbsp;It supports clients in revealing the<br />answers that are already there. The Coach/Client relationship is a partnership.<br />The expert is always you. You know you best!</p><p>The Coach provides a mirror or "shines a light" into new areas through a process<br />of conversation and inquiry.&nbsp;&nbsp;The client gains light bulb moments and perspectives<br />about themselves, which help to move forward past habitual worries, habits and behaviours that have limited or stopped them in the past.</p><p>With the right mix of compassion, loving kindness and non-judgement plus a hefty<br />wallop of kick-ass motivation, the Coach guides you as you begin to visualize and connect with your inner wisdom.&nbsp;</p><p>Not only do you get a Plus One to help you take baby steps with, you embrace all of your truth and reveal who has long been hiding behind the mask.<br /><br />Cori Capp<br />Divorce Coach<br />Mind Body Spirit Coaching<br />Cori Capp<br />bebers1@rogers.com<br /> </p>
