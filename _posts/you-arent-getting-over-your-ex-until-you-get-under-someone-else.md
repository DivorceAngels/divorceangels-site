<p>Hey readers…. If the last person you slept with was your ex you’re in a jam!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can’t get past your marriage if your only recollection of sexual experiences is still with your ex partner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sweet sexual memories of you and your ex will only leave you heart broken this holiday… so hit the dating scene and move on!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I speak from experience….</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My ex left me reeling when he walked out on me.&nbsp;&nbsp;We were still having great sex at the time so I couldn’t reconcile the heartache and the hot urges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every time I thought of sex with another partner my mind wound up back on memories of my ex.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our tender moments in the bedroom and our familiar touch made me sad and depressed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But after a few dates and some fun nights out I feel like my sexual past with my ex is old news.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Other people play the game differently, making the stakes higher and the feelings even hotter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Exploring new ways and different sensations release the mind and really allow you to escape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I thought sex with my ex was the best but I am actually finding that our sex life was just ok.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I’m not promoting being with anyone you meet but I am saying if you get hot for your date why not just try the sex so you can move past your ex.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Trust me…. It works magically!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jennifer, 51, Markham</p>
