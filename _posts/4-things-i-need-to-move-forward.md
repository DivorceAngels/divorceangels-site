<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Admittedly when I first realized I was about to get divorced it was all I could think or talk about.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Having been blindsided by my husband’s desire to leave and the speed in which he left, I was left scrambling to pick up the pieces.  Emotionally scared and confused by his yearning to jump ship, I was overwhelmed by the need to sort through my next steps.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So I solicited my network.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Mom, dad, siblings and close friends – you were not spared a detail of the shock or the frustration and you were each there to help me move forward and make a plan.&nbsp;Thankful and grateful for this incredible network I relied heavily on all of you!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So I talked and talked and then I talked some more.&nbsp;&nbsp;And then I wouldn’t shut up about my divorce!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes – I noticed the yawns and the slight exasperation at the circling conversations but I kept on going because I knew my group to be fiercely loyal.&nbsp;&nbsp;They did not disappoint - they listened and strategized and stuck by me through it all.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And now that some time has passed I have been asked what I need and want most from friends and family.&nbsp;&nbsp;So at this moment in time I list my top four requests…</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li>Check in – Ask me first if I want to talk about my Divorce.&nbsp;&nbsp;If I look worn down or if we are talking about it for too long tell me to shut up and move the topic along – hold that mirror up because it can be all consuming!&nbsp;&nbsp;Please don’t let me become one of those women who rants at nauseam.&nbsp;&nbsp;Help me see there is more to life than this topic of discussion!</li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>Normalize – Help me get back out there so that I feel a little more normal. Even though my world is falling apart and so different than I thought it would be,  please push me off that couch and into the real world.  I lost a partner and it hurts no doubt – but lucky for me I am not sick.  I am alive and healthy – there is still a life to be grateful for so kick me in the shins if I complain too much!</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>Talk about yourselves – I feel like I am so self absorbed right now – let’s bring the conversation back to you and your life – let me also be a good friend/ daughter/ person!</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li>Connect with my new tribe – Um no not yet, I don’t want to commiserate.  I want to move forward and be surrounded by positive inspiring people. What happened to me sucks but I don’t need more negativity – I need to move along into a positive zone!</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So that’s where I am at now… maybe next month I will feel differently but right now I want to go forward….</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Anne, 46</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Barrie Ontario</p>
