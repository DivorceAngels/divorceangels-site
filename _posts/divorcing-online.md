<strong>The Old School</strong>

The traditional divorce process is broken. With the average civil trial costing over $56K, it’s no wonder that access to justice is out of reach for the average Canadian. But, money isn’t the only problem. The family justice system is too complex. The rules of the game aren’t easy to understand and so for people that must resort to representing themselves in court, the system is especially broken. Self-represented litigants now constitute anywhere from 50 to 80% of all family law litigants and things don’t seem to be changing anytime soon.

<strong>The New School</strong>

Thistoo is your personal divorce assistant. It is a comprehensive, online family law platform designed to change how people get divorced. With a primary focus on uncontested divorce, it’s Thistoo’s mission to remove as much of the complexity and cost from the process as possible - helping couples to separate amicably and fairly.

If you’re in the early stages of divorce and wondering what to do next, Thistoo can bring transparency to a confusing process. Thistoo’s Support and Asset Calculators can help to determine how much support should be paid and how your marital assets can be fairly divided. To help with negotiating an out-of-court settlement, Thistoo’s Case Comparison tool predicts the outcome of your divorce based on the results of over 58,000 real Canadian divorce trials. It provides a clear picture of the implications of divorce and shows that it’s almost never worth it to fight it out in court.

For those who are ready to amicably separate, Thistoo provides tools to create a lasting separation agreement and file for uncontested divorce in Canada. Creating a separation agreement is the best strategy to getting through divorce as it gives you control over how your divorce in handled. It will outline all of the terms and conditions of your divorce, and prove that both parties were able to come to an agreement. Thistoo is designed to walk you through the process of customizing your own separation agreement step-by-step. Once you’ve completed the forms online, Thistoo can generate your documents instantly and you’ll be ready to file with the courthouse.
