<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The holiday season is a time of joy, but for those going through a divorce, it can also bring a mix of emotions. It's essential to recognize that happiness is still within reach, and by embracing a positive mindset and some simple strategies, you can make the most of this festive time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here are some tips to help you get through the season:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol>
<li>Celebrate YOU: The holidays are the perfect time for a little self-love and appreciation. Treat yourself to something special – be it a cozy night in with your favorite movie, a spa day, or indulging in a wonderful meal. Remember, celebrating your own company is a wonderful way to stay happy during the holidays.</li>
<li>Create New Traditions: The beauty of the holiday season lies in its ability to create memories. Embrace this opportunity to start new traditions that are uniquely yours. Consider activities like trying out new recipes, finding a new hobby. or even taking a spontaneous trip. These positive changes can add a fresh perspective to the season.</li>
<li>Lean on Your Support System: Family and friends are there for a reason – to support you during both the highs and lows. Share your feelings, and don't be afraid to lean on your loved ones. Surrounding yourself with a strong support system can make the holidays feel happier and connected.</li>
<li>Enjoy the Simple Joys: Holidays don't always have to be grand to be enjoyable. Take pleasure in the small, everyday moments – a cup of hot chocolate, laughing with friends, or the beauty of holiday lights. Focusing on these simple joys can elevate your spirits and make the season more uplifting.</li>
<li>Set Realistic Expectations: Give yourself the gift of realistic expectations. It's okay if things don't go as planned, and it's okay to say no to certain commitments. Allow yourself the freedom to navigate the season at your own pace, making room to find your happiness in the midst of change.</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->
