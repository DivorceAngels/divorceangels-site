<p>If you are getting divorced, there are several important areas you may want to research to better understand the process and your rights:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol>
<li>Divorce laws: Familiarize yourself with the divorce laws in your state, as these can affect various aspects of the divorce process, such as property division, spousal support, and child custody.</li>
<li>Alternative dispute resolution: Consider researching alternative forms of dispute resolution, such as mediation or collaborative divorce, which can often be faster, less expensive, and less stressful than traditional divorce proceedings.</li>
<li>Property division: Understand how property and assets will be divided during the divorce. This includes both marital property (property acquired during the marriage) and separate property (property owned prior to the marriage).</li>
<li>Spousal support: Learn about the factors that are considered when determining spousal support, such as the length of the marriage, the age and health of each spouse, and the earning potential of each spouse.</li>
<li>Child custody: Research the laws and best practices for determining child custody, including joint custody, sole custody, and physical and legal custody.</li>
<li>Divorce forms and paperwork: Research the forms and paperwork required for a divorce in your state, and understand the timeline for filing and processing the divorce.</li>
<li>Hiring a divorce attorney: If you are considering hiring a divorce attorney, research the different types of attorneys, their fees, and how to find a qualified attorney who can help you navigate the divorce process.</li>
</ol>
