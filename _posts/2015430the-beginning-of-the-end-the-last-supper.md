<p>I will never forget the conversation that took place on our couch.</p><p><br />It was like a bachelor episode.&nbsp; It&nbsp; started with "You are a great&nbsp;person."&nbsp; "You mean the world to me and "I will&nbsp;Always love you" BUT the marriage is over.&nbsp;</p><p>The beginning of the end.<br /><br />Our situation was a mutual decision but someone had to initiate&nbsp; "the&nbsp;talk "and the hard core truth that the marriage had come to an end.&nbsp; All&nbsp;the outside efforts to get help was not going to glue this thing&nbsp;together not even "gorilla glue "<br /><br />So now what ?? What do we tell the kids what do I tell my friends and&nbsp;what do I tell my parents?? What do I do ?<br /><br />Packing up the marriage and keeping it going in the house&nbsp; we bought&nbsp;for our "forever life "was going to be a challenge.&nbsp; He moved out.&nbsp; The&nbsp;journey began ......<br /><br />&nbsp;I can truly remember the first night alone in the house with the kids. &nbsp;There was a sense of freedom and a sense of real terror.&nbsp; I would&nbsp;pretend he was on a ski vacation and that the kids and I were just&nbsp;carrying out our daily routine. "Business as usual "<br /><br />Three weeks later the ski vacation was over and so was the illusion&nbsp;that the longing for each other&nbsp; was not a reality anymore.<br />Telling the kids was a very scary moment in time.&nbsp; They were so young&nbsp;but they needed to know the truth that Daddy&nbsp; and Mommy were not going&nbsp;to be living together in the same house ever again.&nbsp; We were advised to&nbsp;be as honest as we could be without giving them hope that this was a<br />phase.&nbsp; We also told them they would be very loved and even though Daddy&nbsp;and Mommy were going to have two separate homes they would be the luckiest kids ever.&nbsp; "You will<br />have two rooms and two beds each."&nbsp; This created a positive spin at a&nbsp;negative time in all of our lives.&nbsp; They bought it !!! and we lived up to the reality of our goals.&nbsp; We were definitely on the same page in&nbsp;painting how things were going to be from this point on.&nbsp; &nbsp; My three&nbsp;year old would cry to my friend&nbsp; "My Mommy and Daddy are not going to be&nbsp;together anymore." Those words were not easy to hear. They were&nbsp;actually heart breaking.<br /><br />Life with my kids and co-parenting with my ex husband was a lot easier&nbsp;knowing that we were both on similar pages on what our reality was.&nbsp; I&nbsp;can't say it was extremely smooth at all. I experienced major bumps&nbsp;along the way . There were nights where I felt I could not be a single<br />mom and that I could not cope with even the thought of being alone and&nbsp;or a vision of a new life with anyone else.&nbsp; All of things ailed me and&nbsp;kept me from getting back into life. I relied on my friends and family&nbsp;to help me through&nbsp; this very dark and transitional period.&nbsp; This took<br />time. This took steps. This took chances And failures and eventually&nbsp;successes.&nbsp; There are many emotions and stages I went through.&nbsp; I do&nbsp;believe that the focus of keeping the kids as the #1 priority and having&nbsp;an ex husband that has been an extremely involved father who was and is<br />dedicated in every way, made this transition a much easier one.&nbsp;<br />I would learn to cope with my anxieties and the loss of my marriage as&nbsp;time would allow me that grieving time to process my new emerging life.<br /><br />Time flew by.&nbsp; Living with my kids alone was a wonderful time in my&nbsp;life. &nbsp;I loved my time alone with them and loved my time when they were&nbsp;with their dad.&nbsp; I switched my whole attitude and found the silver&nbsp;lining in all of this. A new purpose.&nbsp; To this day my boys have never<br />felt the effects of divorce.&nbsp; Seamless&nbsp; it wasn't but successful it was. &nbsp;They don't remember us together but they don't remember anything&nbsp;negative that transpired.&nbsp; The honesty and the consistency in how we raised them apart was the main reason they are children of a successful<br />divorce.</p><p><br />Paula Seligman<br />Peony Design<br /><br /> </p>
