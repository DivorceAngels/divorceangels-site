It can be a time of significant change, affecting every aspect of your life, from your finances and living arrangements to your relationships and sense of self.</p>

<p>Divorce is a legal process that involves dissolving a marriage and dividing assets and responsibilities. It can be initiated by one or both parties and can be contested or uncontested. There are many reasons why individuals may decide to pursue divorce, including infidelity, financial disagreements, and irreconcilable differences.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Regardless of the reason, divorce can be an emotionally challenging experience. It can bring up a range of emotions, including sadness, anger, guilt, and fear. The end of a marriage can also impact children and other family members, making it a complex and difficult process for all involved.</p>
