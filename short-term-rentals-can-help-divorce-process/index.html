Short Term Rentals can help during the Divorce Process

May 25, 2017

Fully furnished rentals are an excellent resource to use during the divorce process. Easily arranged and flexible lease agreements can be the perfect solution when a permanent space of your own is hard to come by. We are here to answer some of your frequently asked questions so you can get more information and feel comfortable about staying in a Toronto furnished rental.

Finding a place to stay during a divorce can be difficult but we make it easy for our clients to book and move into their place a quickly as possible.

Many people are unsure of how long they can stay in a short term rental because the name “ short term” can be misunderstood. Our minimums stays are 1 month, however you are welcome to stay longer than that. We understand that your plans may be uncertain so our leases are flexible and guests are welcome to extend their stay if needed.

Moving can be a stressful and bothersome task especially when you already have so much on your mind. We offer apartments that are completely move in ready, so what can typically be found in our furnished rentals? Our apartments typically include all major furniture items including beds, couches, dining tables and often desks/workspaces. All of our units come with at least one television, cable box, Wi-Fi modem and a DVD player. We also provide linens for the bedroom and bathroom as well as an extra set of sheets for all the beds. You will also find that the kitchen is fully stocked with all utensils, dishes, glasses, cookware, pots and pans. Small appliances such as blenders and toasters are also included in the kitchen, this means you will not have to bring any kitchen utensils if you do not want to or cannot. All of our units are move in ready.

Furniture is of course included in the rent, but many people want to know what else is included in the rent price. There are no hidden costs when renting with us. Included in every stay is unlimited wireless Internet, Rogers VIP cable service and telephone service. All utilities including hydro, gas and water are all included in the rental price as well.

We understand that you may not be alone when looking for a temporary apartment and many clients would like to know if our furnished rentals kid friendly. Of course they are! We understand that children are often the most important consideration during the divorce process so all of our units are child friendly. We have some furnished rentals set up specifically to accommodate families with children however; many of our 2 and 3 bedroom units are ideal for family sharing regardless.

We know it is incredibly important to have buildings close to work or your children’s schools so we will always do our best to place you in the best location for you and your family. We also know pets can be a factor in divorce as well and an important member of the family. When renting a Toronto furnished apartment with us be sure to mention your pet and we will show you the units that we have available that are pet friendly.

There is no need to worry if there are any maintenance issues during your stay as we have a team that will take care of it. We have a help desk that operates 7 days a week to deal with any and all problems that may arise while staying in your suite. Simply contact them via phone or email and we will work to resolve the issue.  In addition, all of our guests have access to the amenities in their given building. The selection of amenities varies between buildings but generally they include, fitness facilities, saunas, steam rooms, conference and party rooms and some even have full sized pools. Our Sky View representatives are happy to give guests all the information they need about the building’s amenities during their stay.

There is no need to stress about move in or move out days because we strive to make them as easy as possible for you. Prior to your stay you will receive an email presenting all the details needed for the check in. This includes time, place and which Sky View representative will be meeting you for the check in. We want all of our guests to be comfortable in their suite so on your check in day we will walk through the unit with you and explain how everything works to help get you settled in. Checkout instructions are simple as well and are provided 2 days before checkout.

All in all our rental application is a quick process, simply talk to one of our booking agents, and fill out a rental form, place your deposit and you are ready to go. This quick process can be a lifesaver when your life is suddenly uprooted by a divorce.

So if you are going through a divorce and are looking for a hassle free, beautiful apartment to rent, be sure to consider renting a Toronto furnished rental.
