Getting Through Your Divorce: Must-Have Products to Help You Heal and Thrive

June 11, 2025

Divorce can feel like the end of the world — but it's really the start of a new one. Healing takes time, but having the right tools around you can bring comfort and calm when you need it most. Whether you’re looking to soothe your mind, organize your life, or just add a little beauty to your day.

Here are our top picks for products that support healing, wellness, and self-care during and after divorce.

💗 1. Emotional Wellness & Self-Care

📘 Journals & Workbooks

  • The Divorce Recovery Workbook – A practical tool for navigating the emotional aftermath of separation.
  • Self-Love Journal: 100 Prompts for Daily Reflection – Build confidence and find clarity through thoughtful writing.

🕯️ Aromatherapy & Comfort

  • Essential Oil Diffuser with Lavender Oil – Transform your space into a sanctuary.
  • Weighted Blanket (10 lbs) – Helps reduce anxiety and improve rest.

🎧 Sound Therapy

  • Sony Noise Cancelling Headphones – Ideal for guided meditations or relaxing playlists.

🏡 2. Reset Your Home Environment

🛏️ Comfort Essentials

  • Bamboo Bed Sheet Set – Soft & Cooling – A fresh bed can symbolize a fresh start.
  • Plush Throw Blanket – Cozy comfort for those much-needed self-care nights.

🖼️ Inspiring Decor

  • Wall Art – "She Believed She Could So She Did" – Empowering and beautiful.
  • Scented Soy Candles – Lavender & Eucalyptus – Fill your space with calming aromas.

📁 3. Stay Organized Through the Chaos

🗂️ Life & Document Management

  • Fireproof Document Organizer – Safely store your important papers.
  • Password Keeper Book – Keep your online life secure and simplified.

📱 Tech Helpers

  • Amazon Fire HD 10 Tablet – Read, journal, Zoom your therapist, or unwind with a show.

🧘 4. Reclaim Your Health & Confidence

🧘‍♀️ Fitness & Movement

  • Yoga Starter Kit – Mat, Blocks, Strap – Gentle movement for strength and calm.
  • Resistance Bands Set for Home Workouts – Exercise at your pace and place.

🥤 Nutrition & Wellness

  • NutriBullet Personal Blender – Make nutritious smoothies to support your energy.
  • Chamomile Tea Sampler – Sip your way to serenity.

📚 5. Books to Empower Your Journey

  • Untamed by Glennon Doyle – Raw, real, and powerfully liberating.
  • The Gifts of Imperfection by Brené Brown – Let go of shame, embrace authenticity.
  • Rebuilding: When Your Relationship Ends – A proven roadmap for emotional recovery.

💬 Final Thoughts

Divorce may bring heartache, but it also brings the opportunity to come home to yourself. These items won’t solve everything — but they can offer comfort, stability, and a sense of control when you need it most.

🌷 You are not starting over. You’re starting fresh — with new strength and new wisdom
