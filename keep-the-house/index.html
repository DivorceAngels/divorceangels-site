What to look at first

Three questions to answer before you keep the house

1

Can you carry it on one income?

The mortgage, property tax, insurance and repairs all land on you. A lender will look at how much of your income that takes up before approving a refinance.

2

What are you trading for it?

Buying out your spouse's share often means giving up other assets, like retirement savings. Equity in a house is hard to spend when you need cash; money in savings is not.

3

What would selling leave you with?

Selling has its own costs, but it can leave you with cash and flexibility. Comparing both paths side by side is the only way to know which one actually leaves you better off.
