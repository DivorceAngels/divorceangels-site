About us

About Divorce Angels

Toronto, Canada · Supporting people through separation and divorce since 2015

Divorce Angels is a Toronto company that helps people through the financial side of separation and divorce. We make Your Divorce Angel, a private app for divorce financial planning and settlement preparation, used in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia.

Where we started

Divorce Angels began in Toronto in 2015 as a confidential divorce concierge. We connected people with vetted lawyers, mediators, financial planners and other professionals, and we built a community where people going through separation could talk openly with others who understood.

That community, Everything Divorce, A Community of Support by Divorce Angels, now has around 45,000 members.

What we do today

Your Divorce Angel helps you understand your financial position before you make decisions that are hard to undo. In one private place, you can:

  • Build a complete picture of your assets, debts, income and expenses
  • Compare settlement scenarios side by side, such as keeping the house, selling it, or an equal split
  • Estimate child and spousal support using the official guidelines where you live
  • Prepare for negotiations with priorities, red flags and questions for your lawyer
  • Organize your documents and have them explained in plain English
  • Ask Angel, our AI guide, questions about your situation at any hour

You can start free, and see how it works or our pricing for more detail.

How we keep it accurate

Support estimates and property rules are based on the jurisdiction you live in. Our divorce financial guides cover every US state and Washington, D.C., every Canadian province and territory, England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland and Australia, and each one cites the legislation it is based on.

Your Divorce Angel provides financial information and guidance, not legal advice. Most people use it alongside their lawyer or mediator, so they arrive prepared and spend their professional time on the decisions that matter. See our disclaimer for details.

Your privacy

Your file is encrypted and visible only to you. We never sell or share your data, and you can delete everything at any time. Our privacy policy explains exactly what we collect and why.
