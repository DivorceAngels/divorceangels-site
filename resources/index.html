Therapy & Emotional Support

You don't have to process this alone

Divorce is one of life's most emotionally demanding experiences. These platforms make professional support accessible, affordable, and available when you need it most.

Why we recommend it: Divorce clients need flexible, affordable therapy. BetterHelp makes it possible to see a therapist from your car, your office, or your couch at 11pm.

The gold standard co-parenting app. Shared calendar, messaging, expense tracking, and an info bank — all in one place. Court-admissible communication records.

Why we recommend it: When communication with your ex is difficult, having everything documented in a neutral platform protects you and keeps things focused on the kids.

2houses

Why we recommend it: When you're ready — and only when you're ready — Bumble's format tends to attract more respectful interactions than other apps.
